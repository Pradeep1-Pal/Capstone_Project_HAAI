### **About NovaTech Innovations**

NovaTech Innovations is a rapidly growing technology company specializing in **AI-driven software solutions** for industries such as finance, healthcare, and retail. Our mission is to empower organizations through **intelligent automation, predictive analytics, and machine learning technologies** that transform how businesses operate and make decisions.

---

### **Position Overview**

We are looking for a **Software Engineer with expertise in Artificial Intelligence** to join our AI Engineering team. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience designing, developing, and deploying machine learning models and intelligent systems that solve real-world problems. You’ll collaborate with data scientists, backend engineers, and product teams to create scalable AI-powered products.

---

### **Key Responsibilities**

* Develop, test, and deploy **machine learning and deep learning models** for production use.
* Build and maintain **data pipelines and ETL workflows** to support AI initiatives.
* Collaborate with data science and product teams to identify and implement **AI-based solutions**.
* Integrate AI models with applications via **RESTful APIs and microservices**.
* Work with **cloud environments (AWS, Azure, or GCP)** to deploy and monitor models.
* Conduct **model optimization** for performance, scalability, and accuracy.
* Research and implement the latest advancements in **AI frameworks and tools**.
* Contribute to **code reviews, documentation, and process improvements**.
* Participate in brainstorming sessions to develop innovative AI-driven features.

---

### **Required Qualifications**

* **Bachelor’s or Master’s degree** in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, or a related field.
* Minimum **3 years of hands-on experience** as a Software Engineer or AI Engineer.
* Proficiency in **Python** and familiarity with frameworks like **TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, or Keras**.
* Strong understanding of **machine learning algorithms, data structures, and software design principles**.
* Experience with **data preprocessing, feature engineering, and model evaluation techniques**.
* Knowledge of **API development**, **containerization (Docker, Kubernetes)**, and **version control (Git)**.
* Exposure to **cloud-based AI deployment** (AWS SageMaker, GCP AI Platform, or Azure ML).
* Strong analytical, problem-solving, and debugging skills.

---

### **Preferred Skills**

* Experience with **Natural Language Processing (NLP)** or **Computer Vision**.
* Familiarity with **MLOps pipelines** and **model monitoring tools**.
* Understanding of **LLMs (Large Language Models)**, **Hugging Face**, **LangChain**, or **OpenAI API**.
* Experience working with **big data frameworks** (Spark, Hadoop).
* Contribution to **open-source AI or data science projects**.
